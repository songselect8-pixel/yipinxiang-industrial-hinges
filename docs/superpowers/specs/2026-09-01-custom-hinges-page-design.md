# Custom Hinges page design specification

Build only `/custom-hinges` as an engineering-led RFQ page within the locked website system. Preserve the Homepage, Products center, product-detail template, Applications page, product data and shared visual language.

The page starts with the approved engineering illustration and a compact custom-manufacturing hero. It then moves from buyer situations, to usable inputs, to original catalog drawing guidance, to a conservative six-stage workflow. A standard-versus-custom comparison and seven explicitly supported product families provide product context. Compact manufacturing and dimensional-check sections use the actual factory exterior as company evidence and the approved process illustrations as supporting scenes. A buyer checklist and source-safe FAQ prepare the final dark navy custom RFQ.

Catalog pages 8–15 are the only product-specific customization sources. Page 18 supports general custom product development and production. Pages 3 and 18 support the compact manufacturing and checking copy. The page does not publish materials, finishes, tolerances, tooling, equipment models, R&D staffing, sample timing, MOQ, capacity, fees, certification or load claims.

No new images are generated. The approved engineering, manufacturing and quality scenes remain labeled as illustrations. Product cards use existing catalog photography; the factory exterior remains the only company-facility evidence. Original drawing assets are displayed unchanged and no fictional dimensions are introduced.

The existing RFQ form is reused with optional labels whose defaults remain unchanged on locked pages. The custom page uses the current accepted file formats and 10 MB client-side preview limit. It does not transmit or store inquiries or files.
