# Products center implementation

The approved homepage is the design reference. Only `/products`, reusable cards, filtering and the product data architecture are in scope. No detail-page routes will be published during this phase.

- Retain ten source-backed families and all 60 original catalog records. Water-drop is a shared profile; 20-A / 20-B and the two flag designs are variants within their families.
- Use exact catalog size/model, family, documented construction and documented application filters. Do not interpret ambiguous compound dimension strings or assign missing units.
- Reuse the homepage card, label, button, table, header and footer styles. Add page-scoped composition for the compact hero, finder, comparison and CTA sections.
- Make View Details useful through an accessible technical preview on `/products`. Keep future clean slugs in data, without linking to unfinished routes. Quote links preserve the selected product and source record in the existing homepage RFQ preview.
- Keep unsupported fields null/empty and omit them from the page. Private contact details and the original PDF remain unpublished.
- Verify source fidelity, intersecting filters, empty/reset states, keyboard/mobile dialogs, inquiry selection, image loading, metadata, navigation and page/table overflow at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390px. Compare the homepage against a pre-change rendering to ensure its locked visual system is preserved.
